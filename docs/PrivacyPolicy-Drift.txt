This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which cladam ("we," "us," or "our") makes the Ilseon Drift mobile application (the "App") available to users (each, a "User").

We are committed to protecting your privacy by ensuring complete transparency about our data practices.

1. DATA COLLECTION

Ilseon Drift is a privacy-first application and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from its users.

The App's design and functionality ensures that no information that can identify you, your device, or your usage patterns is collected by us or any third-party services.

    No Third-Party Analytics/Advertising: The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs (Software Development Kits) that collect data from users.

2. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

Since Ilseon Drift does not collect any personal data, there is no personal data stored on our servers that could be subject to unauthorized access, disclosure, or breach.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Ilseon Drift does not rely on third-party services that collect user data.

4. SHARING AND DISCLOSURE OF DATA

Since we do not collect any personal data, we have no personal data to share or disclose to any third parties, including service providers, law enforcement, or for business purposes.

5. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

Because Ilseon Drift does not collect or retain any personal data about you, the concepts of data access, correction, deletion, or portability do not apply.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Ilseon Drift does not collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.
