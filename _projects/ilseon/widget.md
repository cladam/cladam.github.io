# 📱 The Ilseon Widget

The Ilseon widget brings the app's most critical information directly to your home screen. It is designed for convenience, allowing you to see your next step and record new information without searching for the app icon.

### Current Priority Task

The content of the widget mirrors the **Dashboard** inside the app. It displays the one task you've marked as your current priority. This keeps your immediate goal visible every time you glance at your phone, helping you stay on track without the distraction of a full list.

### Quick Entry Shortcuts

The top right of the widget features three dedicated buttons that act as shortcuts to create new entries immediately:

* **Task:** Opens the input for a new to-do.
* **Idea:** Launches the scratchpad for a quick thought.
* **Voice Memo:** Starts a new voice recording.

By providing these direct paths, the widget ensures that the time between having a thought and saving it is as short as possible.
