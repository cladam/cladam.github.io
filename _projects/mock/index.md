### Why Ilseon?
Ilseon (meaning "Front Line") is built for those who find traditional task managers too bloated. It’s designed for the "capture" phase of GTD (Getting Things Done).

- **Speed First:** Open the app and start typing or speaking immediately.
- **Zero Friction:** No complex hierarchies unless you need them.
- **Open Source:** Transparent, secure, and community-driven.
